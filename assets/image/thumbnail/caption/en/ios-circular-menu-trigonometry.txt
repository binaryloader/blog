Placing and Rotating Circular Menu Items with Trigonometry

How to place and rotate circular menu items using sin, cos, and atan2 in iOS.

📋 What's covered
→ cos and sin are just distances walked along a unit circle
→ iOS flips the y-axis, so you need to negate sin
→ atan2 calculates rotation angle from any touch position
→ Placing items in a circle takes just two lines of trigonometry
