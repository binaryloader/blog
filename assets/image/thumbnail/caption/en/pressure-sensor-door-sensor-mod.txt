DIY Presence Detection with Thin-Film Contact Sensor and Zigbee Door Sensor Mod

Modifying a Sonoff SNZB-04 Zigbee door sensor by wiring a thin-film contact sensor to its reed switch for chair and kitchen mat presence detection automation.

📋 What's covered
→ A $0.50 thin-film sensor turns on lights automatically when you sit down
→ Mod: replacing a Zigbee door sensor's reed switch with a pressure sensor
→ No soldering needed — just jumper wires
→ Applied to both office chair and kitchen floor mat with real usage results
