# Overview

This post covers the concepts of prime numbers and composite numbers.

# Summary

## 1. Natural Numbers

### 1.1. The Number 1

- 1 is neither a prime number nor a composite number.

### 1.2. Prime Numbers

- A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 that has no divisors other than 1 and itself.
- It has exactly 2 divisors.
- 2 is the only even prime number and the smallest prime number.
- Prime numbers can be easily found using the Sieve of Eratosthenes.

### 1.3. Composite Numbers

- A composite number is a natural number greater than 1 that is not a prime number.
- It has 3 or more divisors.
