# Overview

A summary of the new devices and services announced at the Apple September 2021 keynote.

# Summary

## 1. New Device Announcements

- iPad
- iPad Mini
- iPhone 13 & iPhone 13 Mini
- iPhone 13 Pro & iPhone 13 Pro Max
- Apple Watch Series 7

## 2. Existing Service Content Announcements

- Apple TV+
- Fitness+

## 3. Personal Thoughts

- I was hoping for an M1X MacBook, but there was nothing exciting and it felt like a waste of time watching.

# New Devices

## 1. [iPad](https://www.apple.com/kr/shop/buy-ipad/ipad-10-2)

### 1.1. Processor

- Equipped with A13 Bionic
- All chip performance including CPU, GPU, and Neural Engine improved by 20% compared to the previous generation

### 1.2. Input Devices

- 12MP Ultra Wide rear camera with improved low-light focusing
- Center Stage feature applied to the front camera
- Smart Keyboard support and 1st generation Apple Pencil support

### 1.3. Display

- True Tone applied to the display

### 1.4. Connectivity

- LTE support

### 1.5. Storage and Pricing

- Starting at $329 with a minimum storage of 64GB; $299 for education
- Available next week as of September 15

## 2. [iPad Mini](https://www.apple.com/kr/shop/buy-ipad/ipad-mini)

### 2.1. Design

- Redesigned with an all-screen design
- 8.3-inch display size

### 2.2. Processor

- 40% CPU and 80% GPU performance improvement over the previous generation
- Up to 2x Neural Engine performance improvement over the previous generation

### 2.3. Input Devices

- Touch ID moved to the top-right side like the iPad Air (power button location)
- USB-C port applied
- 12MP rear camera with Focus Pixels
- Smart HDR with a new ISP
- 4K video recording capability
- 12MP Ultra Wide front camera with Center Stage
- 2nd generation Apple Pencil support

### 2.4. Display

- Liquid Retina Display applied
- True Tone with up to 500 nits brightness

### 2.5. Connectivity

- 5G support

### 2.6. Pricing

- Starting at $499; available to order as of September 15

## 3. [iPhone 13 & iPhone 13 Mini](https://www.apple.com/kr/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-13)

### 3.1. Design

- IP68 water resistance rating
- Aerospace-grade aluminum frame
- Reduced notch width due to redesigned front TrueDepth camera

### 3.2. Processor

- Equipped with A15 Bionic
- 6-core CPU with 2 high-performance cores and 4 high-efficiency cores
- 4-core GPU
- 16-core Neural Engine capable of processing approximately 15.8 trillion operations per second

### 3.3. Input Devices

- Diagonally arranged dual camera system
- Sensor-Shift OIS applied
- Improved Night mode photography with shorter exposure times
- Cinematic Mode for camera (rack focus technique)

### 3.4. Display

- Ceramic Shield applied to the display
- Super Retina XDR display with up to 800 nits
- Up to 1200 nits for HDR content
- P3 wide color support

### 3.5. Connectivity

- 5G support

### 3.6. Storage and Pricing

- Starting from 128GB with a new 512GB option
- iPhone 13 Mini starts at $699 and iPhone 13 starts at $799

## 4. [iPhone 13 Pro & iPhone 13 Pro Max](https://www.apple.com/kr/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-13-pro)

### 4.1. Design

- Medical-grade stainless steel band for resistance to wear and corrosion
- Reduced notch width due to redesigned front TrueDepth camera
- Ceramic Shield on the front
- Pro: 6.1 inches, Pro Max: 6.7 inches

### 4.2. Processor

- Equipped with A15 Bionic
- 6-core CPU with 2 high-performance cores and 4 high-efficiency cores
- 5-core GPU

### 4.3. Input Devices

- 3x optical zoom for photos and videos
- Night mode on all rear cameras
- Efficient ProRes video encoding for video editing apps

### 4.4. Display

- Super Retina XDR display with up to 1000 nits outdoor peak brightness
- ProMotion applied, automatically adjusting the display refresh rate from 10Hz to 120Hz based on content
  - Frame rate adjusts to match finger speed when swiping apps or scrolling

### 4.5. Connectivity

- 5G support

### 4.6. Storage and Pricing

- 128GB / 256GB / 512GB / 1TB
- Pro starts at $999, Pro Max starts at $1,099
- Pre-orders begin Friday, September 17; all iPhone 13 models launch September 24

## 5. [Apple Watch Series 7](https://www.apple.com/kr/apple-watch-series-7/)

### 5.1. Design

- 20% larger screen compared to Series 6
- 1.7mm bezel, 40% thinner than the previous generation
- Softer, more rounded edges compared to the previous generation
- Front crystal equipped
- IP6X certified and WR50 water resistance rating

### 5.2. Input Devices

- Full-size keyboard allowing tap-to-type or QuickPath swipe typing

### 5.3. Display

- 70% brighter when wrist is down compared to the previous generation

### 5.4. Pricing

- Starting at $399; launching this fall

# New Content

## 1. Apple TV+

- Several new productions to be added in the second half of the year

## 2. Fitness+

- 21 newly supported countries added
- Group Workout feature supporting up to 32 participants
- New workout and meditation content to be added
